About Noviark

Hi, I’m Liam.

I’m Seongchan (Liam) Son, founder and quantitative researcher at Noviark.

I’m studying Applied Finance at Auckland University of Technology and building practical tools, research and educational resources. My interests include financial analysis, quantitative methods and making complex ideas easier to explore.

Noviark brings that work together. Kea Finance NZ is its finance-learning product; the research and project pages link to original work so you can inspect the methods and limitations for yourself.
