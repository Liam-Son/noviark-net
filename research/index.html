Backtesting tools
Experimental

Swift-Quant

A Python walk-forward backtesting engine for selected crypto spot and perpetual markets, with trading costs, funding, concentration controls and reporting.

Requires the reader's own market-data input. A backtest is a research exercise, not evidence of future returns or a live trading service.
